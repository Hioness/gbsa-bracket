How the GBSA Works

  1. Regular season: 5 teams, 4 games each — every team plays 4 of the other 4 (10 total games).
  2. Seeding: ranked by wins; ties broken by total point differential (points scored minus points allowed).
  3. First round: the 1st seed has a bye. 2nd seed plays 5th seed; 3rd seed plays 4th seed.
  4. Semifinals: the two first-round winners join the 1st seed in a three-way round robin — each plays the other two once (3 games total: A vs B, B vs C, C vs A).
  5. Finals: the two teams with the best point differential in the semifinal round robin advance. Finals are best-of-3 — first to 2 wins takes the crown.
